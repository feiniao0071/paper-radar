- novelty_score: 5 means the abstract states a clearly differentiated model, dataset, discovery, capability, experimental loop, or scientific mechanism; 3 means a meaningful but incremental application; 1 means novelty cannot be established from the supplied metadata.
- method_value_score: 5 means the method, data, workflow, or result could directly improve the laboratory's discovery, simulation, experiment, characterization, or research-agent workflow; 3 means broadly transferable; 1 means little actionable value.
- evidence_score: 5 means the abstract provides concrete methods plus quantitative benchmarks, validation, experimental confirmation, or a specific discovered result; 3 means it states a clear method and result with limited detail; 1 means evidence is absent from the supplied metadata. This measures evidence visible in the abstract, not ultimate correctness.

Use the full scale and calibrate strictly. Most relevant papers should receive an eventual medium priority.
